The adaptive result is calculated using your personal baseline, not just the highest raw probability. Why might this differ?
Your baseline is measured during calibration. For each emotion: spike = raw% − baseline%. Sad & Angry receive a 1.5× sensitivity boost. The emotion with the highest spike wins — unless all spikes are below 10, in which case the result is neutral. A 3-frame majority vote stabilizes the final output. This means the raw panel may show a higher probability for one emotion while the adaptive result shows another.
